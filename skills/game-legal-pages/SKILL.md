# Game legal and contact pages

Create clear, game-specific pages grounded in the actual product. Match the existing site's Markdown, navigation, links, and formatting; preserve unrelated content and user edits. Adapt the scope to the request rather than replacing every page by default.

Use consistent, descriptive headings and date formatting across games for equivalent sections, while keeping sections that are specific to a game. Place revision dates near the top. Preserve verified effective dates; do not invent one or treat a revision date as the original effective date.

## Gather facts

Inspect the game's existing pages, relevant app source and configuration, and any supplied release or console information. Distinguish verified implementation from assumptions about the shipped build, third-party SDKs, and dashboard settings. A selection for one game does not establish another game's audience or configuration.

Ask concise, grouped questions early for facts necessary to make the requested pages accurate that are not already established. Continue independent work while waiting, and do not ask again for facts already supplied. Depending on the task, establish:

- Game name; actual developer or publisher name; a public, working support and privacy contact (email or form); and intended launch jurisdictions.
- Intended audience and the exact Google Play target age groups, including whether children are included. Ask separately for each game when unknown; age thresholds and applicable rules vary by jurisdiction.
- Accounts, gameplay data and where it is stored; backup, deletion, retention, and support-request handling; purchases, virtual items, rewards, and relevant user features.
- Ads, analytics, SDK versions, mediation partners, data collected and shared, purposes, consent and privacy controls, and the configuration actually used in release builds.

Verify contact links can be opened where possible without submitting a message; opening a form does not prove submissions reach the developer. Do not invent names, emails, audience, legal bases, safeguards, retention periods, or third-party behavior. When critical answers are missing, make supported improvements, clearly identify unresolved facts, and avoid making an unsupported claim or presenting an incomplete draft as ready to publish.

## Draft and check

- Use the verified developer or publisher name, a usable contact route, and a date reflecting the change. Describe local data, backups, third-party processing, choices, and requests only as supported. Describe contact form processing when applicable. State the intended audience only when confirmed; don't imply an age gate or child-specific protections exist without evidence.
- For games with only local progress and no developer backup, state clearly in both the privacy policy and terms that progress is stored on the device, the developer cannot restore lost data, and clearing data, uninstalling, changing devices, or device failure may erase it. Distinguish any device or platform backup from a developer-provided backup, and explain that restoration depends on device settings and is not guaranteed. Verify these facts for each game before applying the wording.
- Align terms with the actual game, rewards, purchases and storage. Preserve mandatory consumer rights and avoid unsupported claims or promises. Explain form and email contact options accurately.
- Check current primary sources for relevant platform requirements and applicable law when assessing privacy, advertising, children's data, or legal claims. Identify conditional requirements and jurisdictions; don't claim that a policy alone establishes legal or store compliance. If children are in the audience, check applicable Families, age-screening and advertising requirements against actual configuration before making claims.
- Compare the app and SDK inventory with the applicable Google Play Data safety disclosures when requested or relevant. Use current SDK provider guidance, account for all release variants and configured partners, and distinguish possible disclosures from verified console answers. Do not claim the console is correct or change it without access and authorization.
- Check links and run the repository's appropriate build or validation. Report the changed pages, verification performed, and material unresolved questions, including whether contact functionality, release configuration, or console disclosures could not be verified. Recommend jurisdiction-specific legal review where needed; do not guarantee compliance.

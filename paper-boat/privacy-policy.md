## About this policy

This policy explains how Kixan (“we”, “us” or “our”) handles information in Paper Boat: River Rush (the “game”) and when you contact us. It covers the mobile app and the browser version where available. You can reach us through our [contact page](contact.html).

You do not need a game account to play. Mobile leaderboards use Apple Game Center or Google Play Games and their player profiles. The game does not require your real name, email address, phone number, precise location or payment details for local play.

## Information saved on your device

The game saves your best distance, completed-run count, coin and ruby balances, owned and selected boat papers, patterns, flags and themes, upgrade levels, custom flag text and color, tutorial progress, and sound, music, volume, graphics and tilt-steering preferences on your device or in your browser. Custom flag text is a local decoration; it is not your leaderboard profile name.

We do not receive or provide a developer backup of this local game data. We cannot restore it if it is lost. Clearing app or browser data, uninstalling the game, changing devices or a device failure may permanently erase your progress, balances and collection.

The Android app opts out of Android's app-data backup. On iOS, a device or platform backup may include app data depending on your settings. Restoration from any device or platform backup is not guaranteed. Leaderboard records are separate from this local data and cannot restore it.

## Tilt steering

If you enable tilt steering, the game uses your device's orientation readings to steer the boat. These readings are processed on your device and are not sent to us or stored as a history of your movements. Your device or browser may ask for permission. You can turn tilt steering off in the game's settings and use touch or keyboard controls instead.

## Leaderboards

Supported mobile versions use Apple Game Center on iOS and Google Play Games on Android. Leaderboard participation uses your signed-in platform player profile. The game sends scores and the relevant leaderboard identifier to the platform service, which associates them with that profile. We do not operate a separate leaderboard server or store your local game progress on our servers.

Apple and Google process player identifiers, scores and service activity under their own policies. Your platform nickname, avatar and scores may be visible to other players according to the service and your profile settings. The Google Play Games SDK also processes analytics and diagnostic information to operate and improve its services. See [Game Center & Privacy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/data/en/game-center/), [Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy) and [Google's Play Games data guidance](https://developer.android.com/games/pgs/data-collection).

Clearing local game data or uninstalling the game does not itself delete scores already submitted to Apple or Google. Those records may remain with the platform, subject to its retention and deletion practices. They do not preserve your coin or ruby balances, unlocked items, upgrades or settings.

Use your platform's profile and privacy settings to manage leaderboard visibility and service data. Google provides [instructions for deleting Play Games data](https://support.google.com/googleplay/answer/9130646). For Apple-held information, use Apple's Game Center settings and privacy controls. You can also contact us to ask what help we can provide with a platform-data request.

## Advertising

The mobile game uses Google AdMob for banner ads, full-screen interstitial ads and optional rewarded ads. You choose whether to watch a rewarded ad for the reward offered in the game. Ads need an internet connection and may not always be available. The browser version does not use the native advertising integration.

Google and its advertising partners may process your IP address, approximate location inferred from it, device and advertising identifiers, device and app details, app and ad interactions, and diagnostic or performance information. They use information to deliver and measure advertising, analyze performance, prevent fraud and, where permitted by your choices and the service configuration, personalize ads. Advertising and leaderboard services process information separately from your locally saved game progress. We do not use a separate analytics or crash-reporting service.

Learn more in [Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy), [how Google uses information from partner apps](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites) and [Google's Mobile Ads data guidance](https://developers.google.com/admob/android/privacy/play-data-disclosure).

### Your advertising choices

The mobile app checks advertising consent status and shows Google's consent message where required. An in-game privacy-choices option lets you reopen the available advertising choices when the service requires it. The choices shown depend on your location and the advertising service's configuration.

Your device may also offer controls to reset or limit advertising identifiers and manage tracking permissions. Available choices depend on your location and device.

## Updates, ratings and external pages

Mobile releases may check a public update file at startup when an update-check URL is configured. The check does not send your saved game data. As with other web requests, the host receives your IP address and request details. If the check fails, the game continues normally.

The mobile game may ask Apple or Google to show a store-rating prompt after completed runs. The store decides whether to show it and handles any rating you submit.

Opening our website, contact form or a provider link makes a request to the relevant hosting service. That service may receive your IP address, browser or device details, and request information under its own privacy practices.

## Contact and support

Our [contact page](contact.html) links to and embeds a Google Form. When you submit it, we receive the information you choose to provide, such as your message, contact details and information about your device or issue. We use it to respond, investigate problems and handle support or privacy requests.

Google processes information associated with loading and using the form, including technical request information, under [Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy). Please avoid including sensitive information unless it is needed to resolve your request.

**Support-message retention:** We keep support messages and form submissions indefinitely, subject to deletion requests and applicable law. Use the contact form to request deletion of information you have sent us.

## Security, sharing and international processing

Local game data uses your device's local storage and is not encrypted by the game. Protect access to your device and any backups. The contact form is served over HTTPS; Google handles its hosting and transmission. No method of storage or transmission can guarantee complete security.

Advertising, leaderboard, app-store, website-hosting and support providers handle information they receive under their own practices. They may process it in countries other than your own. Their retention periods and privacy controls apply to information they hold; deleting local progress does not automatically delete provider-held information or backups.

## Your privacy choices and rights

You can remove local game data through your device or browser controls or by uninstalling the app. This may permanently erase progress and does not necessarily delete backups or leaderboard records.

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to request access to, correction or deletion of personal information, to object to or restrict certain processing, to withdraw consent, or to complain to a privacy regulator. Use our [contact page](contact.html) for requests about information you sent us. We may need information to understand or verify your request and will respond as required by applicable law. For provider-held information, you can also use that provider's controls and request channels.

## Children and teenagers

Paper Boat: River Rush is intended for players aged 13 and over and is not directed to children under 13. Age-related privacy requirements vary by location. A parent or guardian with questions about a young player's information, or who believes a child under 13 has sent us personal information, can use our [contact page](contact.html) to request help or deletion.

## Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the game, its services or applicable requirements change. We will post the revised version here and provide notice where required by law. The revision date above identifies the latest changes.

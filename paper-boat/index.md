## About the game

Take a little paper boat on an endless river journey. Steer around rocks and logs, ride the currents, collect coins and rubies, and see how far you can travel.

Make the boat your own with different papers, patterns and flags. Spend your earned currency on boat customizations and upgrades, then head back to the river to beat your best distance.

## Your progress

Your progress and settings are saved on your device. Clearing game data, uninstalling the game or changing devices may erase them, and we cannot restore lost progress. Apple Game Center and Google Play Games keep submitted leaderboard records separately; those records do not back up your coins, rubies or collection.

A little paper boat can go a long way. In Paper Boat: River Rush, each trip is a chance to explore the river and beat your best distance.

## Find your way

Steer around rocks and logs, ride the currents, and collect coins and rubies along the way. Keep your attention on the route ahead as you work toward a longer run.

## Make it yours

Try different papers, patterns and flags to give your boat its own look. Use the currency you earn on customizations and upgrades before heading back to the river.

## One more journey

You do not need to set a record on every trip. Get familiar with the river, try a new look, and see how far the next journey takes you.

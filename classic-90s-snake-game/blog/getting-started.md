Classic 90s Snake Game brings a familiar challenge back for another round: guide your snake, collect food, and see how high you can score.

## Keep your next move in mind

Take a moment to plan your route toward the next piece of food. A little room to turn can help you keep a run going as your snake grows.

## Find your look

Earn points as you play and unlock themes and filters. Try a different combination when you want a fresh look for your next round.

## Chase your own best

Start with a score you want to beat and give yourself a few rounds to settle in. Sometimes a short break and a fresh attempt are all you need.

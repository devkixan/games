## About this policy

This policy explains how Dev Kixan, the developer of Block Stack Skybound ("we", "us" or "our"), handles information when you play the game or contact us. You can reach us through our [contact page](contact.html).

## Information stored on your device

The game stores your best score, gameplay statistics, ruby balance, unlocked and selected themes, and sound and vibration preferences locally on your device. We do not back up this game data and cannot restore it if it is lost. Clearing the game's storage, uninstalling it, changing devices or a device failure may permanently erase your progress. Your device or platform may include the data in its own backup, depending on its settings; restoration is not guaranteed.

## Advertising

The mobile game uses Google AdMob to show banner ads and optional rewarded ads. Google and its advertising partners may collect and share information including your IP address and approximate location derived from it, device or advertising identifiers, device characteristics, app and ad interactions, and diagnostic or performance information. They may use this information to provide and measure ads, analyze performance, prevent fraud, and, depending on your choices and their configuration, personalize advertising.

Google describes its practices in its [Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy) and [information about how it uses data from partner apps](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites). You can also learn more about the advertising service in [AdMob Help](https://support.google.com/admob/).

Where an advertising consent form is available, you can make choices there. On mobile, the game's help panel includes **Ad Privacy Options**, which opens the privacy options form when available. Your device may also offer advertising privacy controls. The options available depend on your location, device and the ad configuration.

## Contact and support

If you contact us, we receive the information you choose to submit and use it to respond and handle your request. Our contact page uses Google Forms; Google processes information in connection with providing that service. Avoid sending sensitive information unless it is needed to resolve your request.

## Security

Game progress and preferences are stored on your device using local storage. Protect access to your device and any backups, since someone with access to them may be able to access that information. Our contact page loads its Google Form over HTTPS. Google handles the form and advertising services; see [Google’s Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy) for information about its security practices. Avoid including sensitive information in support requests unless it is needed to resolve your issue.

## Retention, sharing and transfers

Game progress is stored locally until it is removed or overwritten, subject to any device backup. Support messages may be retained for as long as needed to handle requests, resolve disputes and meet legal obligations. Google and any other advertising or support providers handle information under their own applicable terms and retention practices. These providers may process information in countries other than your own.

## Your privacy choices and rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to request access to, correction or deletion of your personal information, or to object to or restrict certain uses. You may also have a right to withdraw consent, appeal a decision, or complain to a privacy regulator. To make a request, use our [contact page](contact.html) or the privacy controls available from the relevant provider. We may need information to verify your request. If you disagree with our response, contact us again to request a review.

You can remove locally stored game data using your device or browser controls, or by uninstalling the game. This may permanently erase your progress and does not necessarily delete backups or information held by third parties.

## Children

Block Stack Skybound is intended for users aged 13 and above. Parents and guardians who have questions about a child’s information or wish to request its deletion can reach us through our [contact page](contact.html). Advertising providers may process information as described above; available choices depend on the device and applicable settings.

## Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when our practices or legal requirements change. We will post the revised policy here with its effective date and provide additional notice where required by law.

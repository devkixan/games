Block Stack: Skybound starts with one simple action: tap to drop a sliding block onto your tower. The challenge is keeping each new block lined up as you climb.

## Give yourself room

Any overhang is cut away after a drop, leaving a smaller surface for the next block. Watch the sliding block and aim to line it up with the tower before you tap.

## Find a rhythm

Three perfect drops in a row grow your block and earn a ruby. Focus on the next drop and let those careful placements build into a streak.

## Change the view

Spend earned rubies on themes when you want a different setting for your next run. You can also adjust sound, vibration and reduced motion in the settings to suit the way you play.

## About the game

Block Stack: Skybound is a one-tap block-stacking game about building your way skyward. Time each drop to line up the sliding block with the tower. Any overhang is cut away, so every miss leaves you with less to work with. Keep stacking and see how high you can go.

## How to play

- **Drop:** Tap to line up the block.
- **Stack:** Overhang is cut off.
- **Grow:** Three perfect drops in a row grow your block.
- **Earn:** Every three perfect drops in a row earn a ruby.

## Themes and rubies

Spend rubies in the store to unlock themes, from sunlit Summer and Spring to Blossom, Monsoon, Harvest, Autumn, Winter, Aurora and Polar Night. Rubies are in-game units with no cash value.

## Play your way

Track your personal best and stats, adjust sound and volume, switch vibration on or off, and turn on reduced motion for calmer menus.

## Ads and your data

The mobile game shows a banner ad and occasional full-screen ads. Watching an optional rewarded ad can earn bonus rubies or a second chance after a game over. Your best score, rubies, themes and settings are stored on your device only. We do not back them up and cannot restore them if you clear data, uninstall or change devices.
